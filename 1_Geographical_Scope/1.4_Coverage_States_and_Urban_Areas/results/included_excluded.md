# States and urbanized areas included and excluded

**Included:** the study area is the 50 states + DC, and all 51 jurisdictions have tracts in the analysis sample; 486 urbanized areas.

**Urbanized areas:** every urbanized area in the SUHI database has at least one tract in the analysis sample.


**Partly excluded:** 755 tracts in included UAs were dropped for missing SUHI fields, renumbering after 2010, or a small ACS universe (section 1.3).
