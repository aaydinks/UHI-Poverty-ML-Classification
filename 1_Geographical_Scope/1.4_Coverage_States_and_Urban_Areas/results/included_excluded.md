# States and urbanized areas included and excluded

**Included:** all 51 jurisdictions (50 states + DC) have tracts in the analysis sample; 486 urbanized areas.

**Excluded jurisdictions:**

- Puerto Rico: held out of the primary sample and analysed as a sensitivity frame (852 usable tracts).
- American Samoa, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands and the U.S. Virgin Islands: not in the SUHI database.
